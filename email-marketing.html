Our Approach

Email Built Around Revenue

Pair it with our social media automation and AI agents so your marketing works as one system and nobody on your team has to copy data between tools by hand.

Email Types

Campaigns for Every Stage of the Funnel

From first touch to loyal customer. Each email type has one job in your funnel.

✉️

Welcome Sequences

Multi-step onboarding sequences introduce your brand, deliver value and guide new subscribers toward a first purchase.

❤️

Nurture Campaigns

Educational content, insights and stories that keep your brand on people’s minds until they are ready to buy.

⚡

Sales Sequences

Time-sensitive offers, product launches and promotions that turn subscribers into customers.

🔄

Re-Engagement

Win back cold subscribers. Sequences spot inactive contacts and bring them back with targeted messages.

📰

Newsletters

Weekly or monthly newsletters that position you as an authority, keep your audience engaged, and give subscribers a reason to open the next one.

🧾

Transactional

Order confirmations, shipping updates and follow-ups that turn buyers into repeat customers and referral sources.

What’s Included

What’s in Your Email Marketing System

Six pieces.

AI Copywriting

Email copy drafted by AI trained on your voice: subject lines, body copy and CTAs tuned to your audience and goals.

Smart Segmentation

Audiences are segmented by behavior, interests and engagement, so the right message reaches the right person.

Behavioral Triggers

Sequences start from actions like page visits, link clicks, purchases and cart abandonment, and react in real time.

A/B Testing

Ongoing tests of subject lines, send times, content and CTAs. AI identifies what works and scales the winners.

Analytics & Attribution

Track opens, clicks, conversions and revenue per email, so you know which campaigns drive results and which need work.

CRM Integration

Connects with HubSpot, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, ConvertKit and more, so your email data flows into your sales pipeline.
