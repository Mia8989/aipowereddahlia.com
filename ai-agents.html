Understanding AI Agents

What Are AI Agents?

An AI agent is software that understands plain language, makes decisions and takes actions for your business. Unlike a chatbot that follows a script, the agents built by AI Powered Dahlia use large language models to read context, qualify leads, answer harder questions and connect to your CRM, calendar and email.

AI Powered Dahlia is an AI strategy and marketing automation agency founded by Dahlia Imanbay. We build custom AI agents that connect to the tools a business already uses, to capture leads, qualify prospects and schedule appointments, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
